Four things to know before you compare

The points that trip people up most often.

  • Data runs to the day before yesterdayOpened on Thursday, the report holds dispensing up to and including Tuesday, so late pharmacies can land first.
  • Defaults show settled periodsWeekly opens on Week Rank No 2, the last completed week. Month / Quarter / Year opens on the last 12 completed months.
  • Cohort Diff % needs Store on the gridEach store has its own brick. Rolled up across stores, overlapping bricks stop describing a real market.
  • National and Cohort are growth onlyTheir raw totals are never shown, and there is no Cohort Margin comparison at all.
